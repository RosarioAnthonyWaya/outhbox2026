TrekPulse Privacy Policy

Your data, explained. TrekPulse reads health and fitness information with your permission. Step activity syncs to your account for history and leaderboards. Other health readings are displayed on your device.

TrekPulse is a health and fitness mobile application designed to help users track physical activity, monitor progress, participate in challenges, and engage with community fitness features.

We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how your personal information and health-related data are accessed, processed, stored, and protected.

You can revoke health access in your device settings, delete your account, or contact us about your data.

2. Account and community information

We collect information you provide to create and use your account, including your name, email address, username, and chosen profile image.

We process your participation in groups, challenges, rewards, and community features, including content you choose to submit, to provide those features.

We also process authentication credentials, sessions, and verification codes to secure your account.

If you enable notifications, we store a push-notification token to deliver app notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Support correspondence is used to respond to your request.

3. Health and fitness data we access and collect

TrekPulse accesses and collects health and fitness data with your permission.

Sources include Android Health Connect, Apple Health on iOS, and supported phone motion or step sensors. Available records depend on your device, connected apps, and the permissions you grant.

  • Steps and activity history: We read step counts and their dates to track daily movement and progress. We calculate estimated distance and calories from steps; these are estimates, not medical measurements.
  • Other health metrics: The Health screen requests access to heart rate, body weight, sleep, and blood oxygen records. Where supported and authorized, it reads heart rate and weight, sleep sessions on Android, and blood oxygen on iOS to display your health snapshot. Not all metrics are available on both platforms. These additional readings are displayed locally and are not synced to the step-history server.
  • Background access: Where supported and permitted, TrekPulse can read steps while the app is not open to keep activity totals and progress up to date.
Health data sources, purposes, and storage

Steps and activity date: Obtained from Health Connect, Apple Health, or motion sensors for tracking, history, and rankings. Stored on your device and on the TrekPulse server, linked to your account.

Distance and calorie estimates: Calculated from step counts for activity summaries. Stored on your device and on the TrekPulse server.

Heart rate, weight, sleep, and oxygen readings: Obtained from supported health sources for display on your Health screen. These readings are displayed on your device and are not uploaded to the step-history server.

4. How we use and store health data

We use steps to show activity history, goals, streaks, estimated distance and calories, and to calculate challenge progress, achievements, rewards, and leaderboard rankings.

We send daily step totals, the associated date, estimated calories, and estimated distance to our server and store them with your account.

The app also stores activity and synchronization information on your device.

Additional heart-rate, weight, sleep, and blood-oxygen readings are used by the Health screen on your device.

That screen does not upload these readings to the TrekPulse step-history server.

Medical disclaimer: TrekPulse is a general fitness and wellbeing application, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

5. Sharing and advertising

Your profile identity, activity totals, rankings, and challenge or group progress can be visible to other users through leaderboards and community features.

Content you post in a group is visible to its audience.

Hosting, database, and notification providers process information needed to operate TrekPulse.

Our application API is hosted on Microsoft Azure. Service providers may process information outside your country.

The TrekPulse application API is configured in Microsoft Azure's West Europe region.

We may disclose information where required by law.

Additional health readings such as heart rate, weight, sleep, and oxygen are not included in leaderboard data.

Advertising and Google AdMob

TrekPulse includes Google AdMob advertising.

Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertisements and prevent fraud, subject to applicable consent choices.

You can revisit available choices under Settings and privacy → Ad privacy choices.

TrekPulse does not sell health data or use it for targeted advertising.

Health Connect data is used for the fitness features described here, not for credit, insurance eligibility, or data brokerage.

Health records and activity values are not included in TrekPulse's advertising requests.

See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's data practices.

6. Permissions and your choices

You can refuse health access or revoke it in Android Health Connect, Apple Health, or your device's app permissions.

Disabling motion or health permissions limits the corresponding tracking features.

Revoking access stops future access under that permission.

It does not automatically delete activity already saved to your TrekPulse account or records held by Apple Health, Health Connect, or other source apps.

7. Retention and deletion

Account and step activity history are retained while your account remains active so that history and rankings continue to work.

When you complete account deletion, we immediately delete your account and associated data from the active application database, including:

  • Step logs and activity history
  • Account sessions
  • Notification tokens
  • Verification codes
  • Account-linked audit records
  • Messages you sent

We remove group memberships. A group with remaining members can continue under another owner.

You can request account and associated data deletion using the TrekPulse account deletion page .

You can also email hello@outhbox.com for help with deletion or to request deletion of particular information.

We may need to verify account ownership to protect your data.

Removing the app alone does not delete server records.

Data held by your health-data source must be managed separately through that source.

8. Security

TrekPulse uses HTTPS for communication with the TrekPulse API.

Account-specific API operations require authentication.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information.

No storage or transmission system can guarantee complete security.

10. Age eligibility

TrekPulse is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Children under 13 must not create an account.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

Lulu Privacy Policy

Your language learning journey, your privacy. Lulu is your companion for learning African languages. This policy explains how your personal information, learning progress, and interactions are handled.

Thank you for using Lulu, your companion for learning African languages.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and providing transparency about how your personal information is processed.

1. Information we collect

When you use Lulu, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password.
  • Usage data: We collect information about how you interact with the app, including the languages you study, completed lessons, quiz scores, and chatbot conversations.
  • Voice input: If you use speech-to-text features, your voice input is temporarily processed to convert to text but is not stored on our servers.
  • Audio output: The text-to-speech feature allows you to hear chatbot responses. No voice data is stored from this feature.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and personalize your language learning experience.
  • Track your progress and saved learning activities.
  • Improve our services based on usage patterns and feedback.
  • Send essential notifications related to your account or learning progress.

These activities allow Lulu to provide a learning experience that reflects your selected languages, completed lessons, and progress within the application.

3. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: With service providers who support our application operations under strict data protection agreements.
  • Legal obligations: If required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Lulu or its users.

4. Data security

We take data security seriously.

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls.

Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text.

5. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your account information.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time.
  • Control permissions related to microphone access for speech-to-text functionality.

If you have questions about exercising these rights, you can contact the Lulu privacy team using the email address provided below.

6. Children's privacy

Lulu is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age.
